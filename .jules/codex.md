## 2026-08-18 - AsyncLock Entity Disposal and Redundant Artifact Clarification
**Observation:** The root README.md failed to articulate the `Dispose` and `DisposeAsync` mechanisms on the `AsyncLock` entity itself, leading to potential unmanaged resource leaks in pedagogical examples where the `AsyncLock` instance was not disposed. Additionally, a redundant `README-NUGET.md` artifact existed, violating the architectural mandate that the root `README.md` serves as the primary epistemological interface.
**Strategic Action:** Deprecated the redundant `README-NUGET.md` file. Updated the primary `README.md` to explicitly list the `Dispose` and `DisposeAsync` methods in the interface specification and corrected all implementation examples to utilize modern C# `using` or `await using` declarations for the `AsyncLock` instances.
